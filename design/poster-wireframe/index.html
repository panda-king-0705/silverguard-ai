Budgets include headings, diagram labels and the short sources strip (everything printed except the QR code). They are set to what measurably fits at 20 pt body with the planned diagrams (density test). The 450-word ceiling would need about 15 pt body, readable only from about 0.8 m; 16 pt fits about 420 words. Team decision: see README.

Type scale for A3 read at 1–2 m

RoleSizeReadable to*
Title72 pt3.8 m
Stat numbers48 pt2.5 m
Hook, zone headings, privacy line32 pt1.7 m
Layer names, diagram labels28 pt1.5 m
Body, quotes20 pt1.05 m
Sources strip, AI note12 pt0.6 m

*Distance at which the x-height reaches 0.2° of visual angle, the typical critical print size for normally sighted readers (Legge & Bigelow 2011, Journal of Vision 11(5):8). Assumes x-height ≈ 0.52 of the font size (system sans fonts), so treat as a guide. Rule of thumb: 2 m needs about 38 pt, 1 m about 19 pt. On A3 only the title and the three numbers carry past 2 m; headings work at about 1.7 m and body text at 1 m.
